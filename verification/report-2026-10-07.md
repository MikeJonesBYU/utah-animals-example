## Usability notes

- **U1 (medium):** After a completed session, plain pages keep showing the thank-you modal until the next `?test`. After a closed tab, plain pages bring back the abandoned session's bar and keep recording clicks into it. The moderator can't browse the site between participants.
- **U2 (medium):** Every `?test` visit saves a session at once, even if nobody clicks Start. That leaves "incomplete, 0 of 10" rows and uses up participant numbers.
- U3 (low): the moderator types `index.html?test` by hand for each participant.
- U4 (low): task 2 says "cougars" but the card says "Mountain lion". This is deliberate, but a give up there may be a vocabulary miss rather than a navigation problem.
- U5 (low): tasks 1 and 10 name the target animal exactly. No task uses a category label.
- U6 (low): the spec doesn't define how unfinished tasks appear in the CSV.
- U7 (low): Back and Forward are timed when the next page finishes loading.
- U8 (low): every download on the same day has the same filename.
